    This prototype holds ten instruments. The full version will hold 15 to 20 across all three families, including the gongs (cồng chiêng) and instruments of more ethnic minorities. Each will have real photographs, real recordings and a filter by ethnic group. For now only the đàn bầu has a photograph; the others show an outline drawing in its place.
