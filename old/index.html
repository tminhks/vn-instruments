Ngũ Âm

Play Vietnamese instruments with your fingers. Turn the sound on.

Play on your phone

Scan with the phone camera to play with your fingers and tilt the phone to bend the đàn bầu. On this computer you can still play with the mouse or keyboard.

tminhks.github.io/vn-instruments

Đàn tranh (zither)

Touch a string, or slide a finger across several strings. Use more than one finger at once.

Đàn bầu (monochord)

Tap the string to pluck it. Then tilt the phone, or drag the slider, to bend the note the way a player bends the rod.

Sounds are made in the browser with the Web Audio API. This is the phone version of the Ngũ Âm prototype (CSCU9X5 student project). Everything can also be played with a mouse or keyboard on the laptop version.
